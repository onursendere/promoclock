The best AI image generator in 2026 depends on what you make: Midjourney for polished artwork, Ideogram for text inside images, Recraft for vectors and Adobe Firefly for commercial work inside Creative Cloud. We track pricing and offers for 100 AI tools, and every price below is as of October 2026. Nearly every tool has a free tier, but free images are often public and not licensed for commercial use.

## Which AI image generator is best in 2026?

No single tool wins every job: Midjourney leads on finished-looking art, while Ideogram, Recraft and Firefly win on text, vectors and licensing.

- **Best overall look:** [Midjourney](/tools/midjourney/), from $10/month, with V8.2 as its default model since July 24, 2026.
- **Best for text in images:** [Ideogram](/tools/ideogram/), whose open-weight Ideogram 4.0 model renders readable typography.
- **Best for logos and icons:** [Recraft](/tools/recraft/), which exports editable SVG files on every plan.
- **Best for commercial-safe work:** [Adobe Firefly](/tools/adobe-firefly/), whose own models are trained on licensed and public-domain content.
- **Best free option:** [Gemini](/tools/gemini/) and [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/), which both create and edit images on free accounts.
- **Best for many models in one account:** [Krea](/tools/krea/), [Freepik](/tools/freepik/) (now Magnific) and [Leonardo.ai](/tools/leonardo/).
- **Best for social templates:** [Canva](/tools/canva/), which turns a prompt into a layered, editable design.

## How do you choose an AI image generator?

Start with the output you need, because photoreal scenes, illustrations, typography, vectors and photo edits each favor a different tool.

- **Photoreal images:** Leonardo.ai's Lucid Realism model and Midjourney's native 2K HD mode target realistic renders, and Krea and Firefly add partner models such as Nano Banana 2.
- **Illustration and concept art:** Midjourney's personalization profiles learn your taste, and Leonardo.ai trains custom models on your own art style.
- **Text in images:** Ideogram places exact headlines on posters and merch, and Layerize Text turns each line into an editable layer.
- **Vectors and brand assets:** Recraft outputs SVG logos and icons, with Figma plugins and CMYK print settings.
- **Editing existing photos:** Firefly's Generative Fill and Generative Expand, or the photo editors covered below.

In the classic Midjourney vs Firefly choice, Midjourney wins on style and Firefly on licensing and Photoshop integration.

## What do the main AI image generators cost?

Most entry plans cost $8–$20 a month; what separates them is credits, privacy and commercial rights.

### Midjourney

Midjourney costs $10/month for Basic, $30 for Standard, $60 for Pro and $120 for Mega, or $8, $24, $48 and $96 a month billed annually, according to [Midjourney's plan comparison](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans). Basic includes 200 minutes of Fast GPU time and no Relax mode; Standard and above add unlimited Relax-mode images. Extra Fast time costs $4 per hour.

### Adobe Firefly

Adobe Firefly Standard costs US$9.99/month with 2,000 credits, Pro US$19.99 with 4,000, Pro Plus US$49.99 with 10,000 and Premium US$199.99 with 50,000, per [Adobe's plans page](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html). Paid plans include unlimited standard image features such as Generative Fill; credits pay for video, audio and partner models like Nano Banana 2. Pro adds Photoshop on web and mobile.

### Ideogram

Ideogram gives free accounts 30 credits a week; Plus costs $20/month for 2,400 credits, Pro $60/month for 7,500 and Team $30 per user (two-user minimum), per [Ideogram's pricing page](https://ideogram.ai/pricing). Yearly billing drops Plus to $15 and Pro to $42. Ideogram 4.0, released June 3, 2026, is a 9.3B-parameter open-weight model built for typography.

### Leonardo.ai

Leonardo.ai's free plan refreshes 150 fast tokens a day, but every free image is public. Essential costs $12/month, Premium $30 and Ultimate $60, or $10, $24 and $48 billed yearly, excluding tax. Unused paid tokens roll into a bank of up to 3 months' worth, and Premium adds unlimited relaxed generation on selected models.

### Recraft

Recraft Basic costs $12/month ($10 billed annually) for 1,000 credits, Pro starts at $20/month for 2,000 credits, and Teams starts at $22 per seat with a two-seat minimum. Vector generation is included on every plan, even Free, and returns editable SVG files. Recraft V4.1, released in May 2026, ships in eight versions, including Vector and Utility.

### Krea

Krea's free plan gives 100 compute units a day; Basic costs $9/month ($5 billed yearly) for 5,000 units and Pro $35/month ($21 yearly) for 20,000. Krea says Pro's units cover about 257 Nano Banana 2 images. Its real-time canvas renders as you type or draw, and its in-house Krea 2 model focuses on style mixing.

### Freepik (now Magnific)

Freepik rebranded as Magnific in 2026, and freepik.com now redirects to magnific.com. Magnific shows local prices: from the UK, Premium is £14/month, Premium+ £32 and Pro Starter £78, excluding VAT. Premium+ and Pro generate with most image models without spending credits, subject to fair use, and the subscription also covers a 250M+ asset stock library.

### Canva

Canva Free includes up to 20 uses of Standard or Premium AI tools a month. Canva Pro costs US$144/year and raises that to an estimated 200 Premium or 20 Ultra AI uses a month; Business costs US$250/year per person. Heavy users can add AI Pass at US$100 per person per month.

### ChatGPT

ChatGPT creates and edits images inside the chat on every plan, with limited, slower generation on Free. Go at $8/month raises image limits but may show ads, Plus costs $20/month, and Pro, from $100/month, offers unlimited, faster image creation under abuse guardrails. ChatGPT suits quick visuals inside a conversation more than design production.

### Gemini

Gemini generates and edits images free with a Google Account, and paid Google AI plans bundle Google's Nano Banana image models with higher limits. Google AI Plus costs $4.99/month for double the free limits, and Google AI Pro costs $19.99/month for 4x the free limits plus 1,000 Google Flow credits.

## Which AI image generators allow commercial use?

Most paid plans allow commercial use, but many free tiers don't, and each vendor words its rules differently.

- **Midjourney:** subscribers on every plan can use images commercially, but companies with more than $1,000,000 in yearly gross revenue must be on Pro or Mega.
- **Adobe Firefly:** Adobe says outputs from its Firefly models can be used for commercial purposes; checking partner-model output is your job, and IP indemnification is reserved for eligible enterprise customers.
- **Ideogram:** Ideogram says it doesn't claim ownership of generated images, which you may use for any purpose, including commercial use, under its terms.
- **Leonardo.ai:** commercial use is allowed on free and paid plans, but public images, including all free-plan images, give Leonardo perpetual rights to use them.
- **Recraft:** paid plans include full ownership and commercial rights; free-plan images belong to Recraft.
- **Krea:** Pro, Max, Business and Enterprise include a commercial license; the Free plan does not.
- **Freepik (Magnific):** every paid plan includes a commercial license, and selling physical merchandise requires Pro.

For ChatGPT, Gemini and Canva, read each vendor's terms before using AI images in client work.

## What are the best AI photo editing tools?

For editing photos rather than generating them, Photoroom and Pixelcut lead on product shots, Picsart on mobile edits and Remini on restoring old or blurry pictures.

- [Photoroom](/tools/photoroom/): Pro costs $12.99/month or $89.99/year with 8,000 AI credits, and Background Remover doesn't use credits on paid plans. The free plan is for personal use only.
- [Pixelcut](/tools/pixelcut/): Pro costs $10/month ($8 billed yearly) with 600 credits and a commercial license, and covers a 3-person team. Free exports carry no watermark.
- [Picsart](/tools/picsart/): the free editor covers standard photo and video edits; from the UK, Pro is listed at £14/month or £7/month billed yearly.
- [Remini](/tools/remini/): unblurs and restores old photos; app prices vary by account, and Remini Web Business adds commercial use.
- [remove.bg](/tools/remove-bg/): the standalone site closes on December 1, 2026, with background removal moving to Canva and the API to Leonardo.ai.
