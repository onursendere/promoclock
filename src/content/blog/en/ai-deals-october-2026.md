## What changed for Claude in October 2026?

Claude got monthly API credits on Max and Team, a new Haiku model, a temporary usage discount for artifact work, a cloud credit for Claude Code and a startup offer that filled up within days. Peak hours and weekly limits stayed the same.

### Max and Team: $100–$500 a month in API credits

Since October 7, 2026, [Claude Max and Team include monthly API credits](/deals/claude-max-team-api-credits-2026/): $100 on Max 5x, $200 on Max 20x, and $20 per Standard seat and $100 per Premium seat on Team, pooled up to $500, according to [Anthropic's Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17154008-monthly-api-credits-for-max-and-team-plans). Free, Pro and Enterprise plans don't get them. Our [API credits guide](/blog/claude-max-team-api-credits/) walks through the claim.

- You claim the credits once, after seven days on the plan, by linking a Claude Console organization under Settings > Billing on claude.ai.
- They cover the Claude API, Message Batches, the Console Playground, Claude Managed Agents and the Agent SDK, not interactive Claude Code or extra usage.
- Credits refresh every billing cycle and expire at its end, with no rollover.
- The same day, Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 per 1M input tokens on prompts up to 100,000 tokens and halved Sonnet 5.5 cache reads to $0.10 per 1M tokens.
- Open-source maintainers can apply for [6 months of Claude Max 20x](/deals/claude-for-open-source-max-2026/), and since October 8 Anthropic's OSS Scanner offers free security scans to established projects.

### Claude Code cloud credit: spend it before November 4

The claim window for Claude Code's cloud-session credit closed on October 7, 2026. Individual Pro subscribers who claimed it got $100 and Max subscribers $250 for Claude Code sessions that run on Anthropic's servers. Unused credit expires on November 4, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT, and it covers cloud sessions only, not local Claude Code, chat or Cowork.

### Claude for Startups: closed to new applications

On October 6, 2026, Anthropic added a free year of Claude Team for up to five seats and $1,000 in API credits to Claude for Startups. Within days, Anthropic said both offers were over capacity. As of October 14, the program page says the current cohort is full and Anthropic isn't accepting new applications; it will announce the next cohort in its newsletter. Startups that already claimed the offers keep them, as our [Claude for Startups entry](/deals/claude-for-startups-team-year-2026/) explains.

### What stayed the same

Claude peak hours still run on weekdays from 13:00 to 19:00 UTC, when 5-hour session limits on Free, Pro, Max and Team drain faster. Claude Code weekly limits have stayed 25% above the old baseline since September 14, 2026. ## What's new with ChatGPT and Codex in October 2026?

GPT-5.5 retired from ChatGPT and Codex on October 14, 2026, and ChatGPT Pro now comes in three usage tiers: $100, $200 and $500 a month.

- **GPT-5.5 retirement:** on October 14, 2026, GPT-5.5 left ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on all plans, according to [OpenAI's model docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models). The API is unaffected. OpenAI points Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users to GPT-6 Sol, and Free and Go users to GPT-6 Luna in the desktop app.
- **Plans:** Free costs $0, Go $8/month in the US and Plus $20/month. Plus adds GPT-6 advanced reasoning models, expanded deep research, more Codex usage and no ads.
- **Pro tiers:** the $100 tier gives 5x Plus usage, the $200 tier reopened to new sign-ups on September 29, 2026, and the new $500 tier has the most usage. Pro also adds Dot, an always-on agent.
- **Pro $200 change:** according to press reports, the $200 tier now includes 10x Plus usage instead of 20x. Existing subscribers keep the old allowance until October 29, 2026.
- **Codex models:** OpenAI recommends GPT-6.1 Sol for complex coding and agentic work and GPT-6 Luna for focused, repeatable tasks; GPT-6 Astra is its most capable model.
- **Open-source maintainers:** the [Codex for Open Source program](/deals/codex-for-open-source-chatgpt-pro-2026/) gives approved maintainers six months of ChatGPT Pro with Codex, and projects can apply for API credits from a $1 million fund.

US college students have until October 31 to claim 4 free months of ChatGPT Plus, worth $80. Plan-by-plan details are on our [ChatGPT page](/tools/chatgpt/) and [Codex page](/tools/codex/).

## What did Google change for Gemini in October 2026?

Google released Gemini 4 Argon, its new flagship reasoning model, in the Gemini API on October 13, 2026, and keeps Gemini 3.8 Flash at half price until December 31. Its two consumer giveaways, a free student year and the Pixel 10 Pro bundle, are still open.

- **Gemini 4 Argon:** generally available in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio since October 13, 2026, according to [Google's release notes](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog). It costs $2 per 1M input tokens and $10 per 1M output tokens through November 30, 2026, then $4 and $20 from December 1; Batch and Flex requests cost half. It has a 1M-token context window and isn't available on the free tier or with the Google AI Pro plan.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash API:** $0.75 per 1M input tokens and $3.75 per 1M output tokens on the paid Standard tier until December 31, 2026, according to [Google's Gemini API pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). On January 1, 2027, the rates double to $1.50 and $7.50. Context caching is also half price until the end of 2026, and the free tier stays free. Gemini 3.7 Flash was deprecated on October 8, and its API requests now route to 3.8 Flash.
- **Free Gemini app users:** since October 9, 2026, people without a Google AI plan default to Auto, which sends most prompts to Flash-Lite and harder ones to Flash or Pro, according to [Google's Gemini help center](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). Google AI Plus subscribers get an email with their own date.
- **Students:** eligible US college students get a [free year of Google AI Pro](/deals/google-ai-pro-students-2026/), normally $19.99/month, and students in 140+ other markets get a free year of Google AI Plus. Redeem by December 31, 2026.
- **Pixel buyers:** a new Pixel 10 Pro, Pro XL or Pro Fold includes 12 months of Google AI Pro, and that offer ends on October 31, 2026. Pixel 11 Pro models include 6 months instead.

Gemini itself stays free with a Google Account. In the US, Google AI Plus costs $4.99/month, Google AI Pro $19.99/month and Google AI Ultra $99.99 or $199.99 a month. Developers can try most Gemini models free in [Google AI Studio](/tools/google-ai-studio/).
