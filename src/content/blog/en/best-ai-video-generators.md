- **Best editors:** [CapCut](/tools/capcut/) for free short-form edits, [Descript](/tools/descript/) for transcript editing and [OpusClip](/tools/opusclip/) for Shorts.

## Which text-to-video model should you use?

Pick Runway or Luma for directed, production-grade shots, Kling or Hailuo AI for native audio on a budget, and Google Flow to use Veo 3.1 free.

### Runway

Runway Standard costs $15/month for 625 credits, Pro $35 for 2,250 and Max $95 for 9,500, with 20% off on annual billing, according to [Runway's pricing page](https://runway.com/pricing). Max, rolled out on June 1, 2026 to replace Unlimited, adds ProRes, HDR and one month of credit rollover.

### Kling

Kling Standard costs $10/month for 660 credits, Pro $37 for 3,000, Premier $92 for 8,000 and Ultra $180 for 26,000, and yearly billing is 34% off. Kling 3.0 generates video with native audio and multi-shot storyboards, and native 4K needs a subscription. Monthly plans often show first-month discounts, so check the renewal price.

### Google Flow (Veo 3.1)

Google Flow gives 50 credits a day to anyone 18+ with a Google Account. Google AI Plus ($4.99/month) adds 200 monthly credits, AI Pro ($19.99) 1,000, and AI Ultra ($99.99 or $199.99) 10,000 or 25,000. Per [Google's Flow help center](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234), Veo 3.1 Lite costs 10 credits per video, Fast 20 and Quality 100, and Ultra pays half on Lite and Fast. Video editing and Avatars aren't available in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK.

### Luma (formerly Dream Machine)

Luma has no free plan, only trial credits for new accounts. Plus costs $30/month for 10,000 credits, Pro $90 for 40,000 and Ultra $300 for 150,000. Luma retired the Dream Machine name, and its Ray3.2 model, released June 9, 2026, renders 1080p clips up to 20 seconds with up to 16 keyframes and HDR EXR export.

### Hailuo AI

Hailuo AI Standard lists at $14.99/month for 1,000 credits, with Pro at $54.99, Master $119.99 and Max $216; yearly billing saves up to 47%. Its MiniMax H3 model, released August 3, 2026, makes 4–15 second clips at up to 2K with native stereo audio, and its weights are open.

### Pika

Pika's Free plan includes 0 monthly credits, so you buy packs from $25 for 1,500 credits or subscribe: Starter costs $10/month for 900 credits, Creator $35 for 3,150 and Fancy from $95. Creator is the cheapest plan with a commercial license. Pika 2.5 runs next to partner models such as Seedance 2.5 and Veo 3.1.

### PixVerse

PixVerse's free Basic plan gives 60 starter credits plus 30 a day, capped at 540p with a watermark. Standard costs $10/month, Pro $30 and Premium $60, or 20% less yearly. PixVerse V6 generates 15-second 1080p clips with native audio, and Off-Peak Mode cuts credit use by 30% on Pro and 50% on Premium.

### Higgsfield

Higgsfield Starter costs $19/month for 270 credits, Plus $59 for 1,200 and Ultra from $129 for 3,000; annual billing lowers Plus to $47 and Ultra to $99. Cinema Studio 4.0 stacks named camera moves, and Plus and Ultra include credit-free Unlimited models for set periods.

## What is the best Sora alternative?

OpenAI discontinued Sora, so the closest alternatives are Veo 3.1 in Google Flow, Kling 3.0, MiniMax H3 in Hailuo AI and PixVerse V6, which all generate video with sound.

[OpenAI's help center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation) says the Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026, and the Sora API on September 24, 2026. Several multi-model platforms listed Sora 2 before the API closed, so check their current model menus.

- **Free to try:** Google Flow's 50 daily credits cover five Veo 3.1 Lite videos.
- **Cheapest subscription:** Kling Standard at $10/month.
- **Open weights:** MiniMax H3 can also be self-hosted.
- **Inside a chatbot:** Google AI subscribers aged 18+ can create and edit videos by chatting with Gemini Omni in [Gemini](/tools/gemini/).

## Which AI avatar video tool is best?

HeyGen is the better pick for marketing and translated videos, while Synthesia fits corporate training; both start at $29/month.

### HeyGen

HeyGen's free plan makes 3 videos a month, each up to 1 minute, with a watermark. Creator costs $29/month for 600 credits, Pro $49 for 1,000 credits and 4K export, and Business $149 plus $20 per extra seat; annual billing saves about 17%. Avatar IV costs 16 credits per minute with a Photo Look and 31 with a Video Look, and lip-synced translation covers 175+ languages.

### Synthesia

Synthesia's free Basic plan includes 500 credits a month, about 10 minutes of video or dubbing, but Basic users can't download videos. Starter costs $29/month ($18 yearly) for 1,250 credits, and Pro, formerly Creator, costs $89/month ($64 yearly) for 6,000 credits plus API access. Dubbing costs 120 credits a minute, or 240 with lip sync.

## Which AI video editors and clip tools are worth paying for?

Use CapCut for free short-form editing, Descript for transcript-based editing and OpusClip to cut long videos into Shorts; the rest suit narrower jobs.

### CapCut

CapCut is free to edit and export unless a project uses Pro templates, effects or AI features. CapCut's own guide lists Pro at $19.99/month or $179.99/year, though prices vary by region and app store. Its terms give ByteDance a perpetual license to content you submit, which matters for client work.

### Descript

Descript's free plan includes 60 media minutes a month and 100 one-time AI credits, with 720p watermarked exports. Hobbyist costs $24/month ($16 yearly), Creator $35 ($24) and Business $65 ($50). Editing the transcript edits the video, and the Underlord co-editor cuts clips from a prompt.

### OpusClip

OpusClip charges 1 credit per minute of source video. Free includes 60 credits a month with watermarked clips; Starter costs $15/month, and Pro $29/month or $14.50/month billed annually at $174. OpusClip picks moments, adds captions and reframes speakers vertically.

### Captions

[Captions](/tools/captions/), built by Mirage, has a free version for basic editing. Its listed iOS prices put Max at $24.99/month for 500 credits and Frontier tiers at $69.99–$279.99. Unused credits roll over for up to two extra months.

### VEED

[VEED](/tools/veed/)'s free plan exports 720p video up to 10 minutes with a watermark. VEED shows local prices: from the UK, Creator is £20 per user per month, roughly halved on yearly billing. Paid plans include unlimited auto subtitles within fair use.

### InVideo AI

[InVideo AI](/tools/invideo/)'s free credits reset every Monday. Starter costs $20 per seat a month for 400 credits, Plus $60 ($36 annually) and Max $150 ($75 annually). Its Agent Two plans shots and picks models, and timeline editing uses no credits.

### Pictory

[Pictory](/tools/pictory/) has no free plan, only a 14-day trial with 15 video minutes. Starter costs $29/month for 200 exported minutes, Professional $59 for 600 and Team $199 for 1,800. It turns scripts, URLs and slides into captioned stock-footage videos.
