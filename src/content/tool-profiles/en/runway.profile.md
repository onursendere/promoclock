## What is Runway?
Runway builds generative models and the tools to use them. Runway Creative is the app for video, image and audio work; Runway Dev is the API platform; Runway Robotics uses the GWM-1 world model for photorealistic simulation. The main site now lives at runway.com.

## What stands out
- **One subscription, many models.** Runway's own Gen-4.5 sits next to third-party models, so you can compare outputs without separate accounts.
- **Built for production handoff.** 4K upscaling, brand kits and, on Max, ProRes and HDR suit agency and film workflows.

## Who it's for
Runway suits people who treat AI video as part of a larger production, not a one-click generator. Solo creators can start on Standard, but heavy users will feel the credit limits quickly.

## Limitations
Credits run out fast on premium models: 625 Standard credits cover under a minute of Gen-4.5. Standard and Pro credits do not roll over, Standard is capped at 3 projects, and Unlimited-style relaxed generation is being retired. Free users only see a limited set of models and get watermarked videos.
