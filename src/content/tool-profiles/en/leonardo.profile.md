## What is Leonardo.ai?
Leonardo.ai is a web platform and mobile app for generating, editing and upscaling images and video. Usage runs on tokens: paid plans get a monthly allowance, from 8,500 fast tokens on Essential to 60,000 on Ultimate.

It is part of Canva and also serves teams through Starter and Growth plans, and developers through a pay-as-you-go API.

## What stands out
- Consistency tools: custom model training, consistent characters and Blueprints keep a style steady across a project.
- Relaxed generation on paid tiers covers selected models such as Lucid Origin, Phoenix 1.0, Motion 2.0 and Hailuo 2.3.
- Higher tiers raise concurrency, from 2 simultaneous generations on Essential to 6 on Ultimate.

## Limitations
- Free-plan creations are always public; private generation needs a paid plan.
- Relaxed generations cover only selected models and may slow down when demand is high.
- Downgrading a plan expires any banked tokens above the new plan's limit.
- Prices exclude tax.
