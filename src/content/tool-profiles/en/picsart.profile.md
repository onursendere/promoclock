## What is Picsart?
Picsart began as a mobile photo editor and now runs as a credit-based creative platform. One account covers the iOS and Android apps and picsart.com, mixing manual editing tools with generative models from Picsart and its partners.

## What stands out
- **Many models, one subscription.** The pricing page lists video, image and audio models from Google, Kling, ByteDance, Runway, ElevenLabs and Recraft.
- **Agents that finish jobs.** Named agents such as a reel director, brand designer and listing broker plan the work and hand back assets.
- **Developer hooks.** A command-line tool and an MCP connection put the same editing and generation tools inside coding agents.

## Limitations
Prices are set per country, so the figure you see depends on where you are. Monthly credits expire at the end of each cycle, team seat counts cannot change mid-subscription, and Ultra is not sold through the mobile apps. Free accounts see ads and miss premium content, advanced removal and the larger storage tiers.
