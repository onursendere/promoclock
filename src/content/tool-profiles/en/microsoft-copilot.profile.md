## What is Microsoft Copilot?
Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's assistant for chat, images, files and work tasks. It runs at copilot.com, in desktop apps for Windows and Mac, and in iOS and Android apps. Sign in with a work account and it switches to your organization's Copilot; personal and work data stay apart.

## What changed in 2026
- The Microsoft 365 Copilot app became simply the Microsoft Copilot app, and consumer chats and content merged into it.
- Podcasts, Group Chat and consumer Deep Research retired on August 18, 2026.
- Copilot Tasks moved to Cowork; Personal and Family subscribers can view old tasks but must upgrade to run new ones.
- Microsoft 365 Pro now tops the individual lineup at $99.99 a month with the highest AI limits.

## Limitations
AI features belong to the subscription owner and can't be shared, and Microsoft 365 Basic has none. Every plan has usage limits and AI credits that pause features until they reset. Pro bills monthly only, and Cowork's remote browser isn't ready — web actions run through Microsoft Edge on your own device.
