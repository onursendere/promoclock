## What is CapCut?
CapCut is ByteDance's all-in-one video editor, available as CapCut Online, a desktop app, a mobile app and CapCut Pad for tablets. It sits alongside ByteDance's other creative apps, including Dreamina for AI images and video, Pippit and the Hypic photo editor.

## What stands out
- **Low barrier to start.** No credit card is needed to edit online, and free users can preview most premium features before deciding.
- **Social-first tooling.** Captions, templates and vertical formats are built for short-form platforms.
- **Tiered plans.** A newer Standard plan sits below Pro for people who want templates and assets without the full AI and storage bundle.

## Limitations
- There is no public price list. The same Pro plan can cost different amounts depending on country, device and app store.
- Free users can try premium templates, effects and AI features, but must subscribe to export projects that use them.
- CapCut's help pages are not fully consistent about Pro storage, citing both 100GB and 1TB in different articles.
- The terms grant ByteDance a broad, perpetual license to uploaded content, which matters for client or confidential work.
