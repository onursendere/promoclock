## What is Granola?
Granola works like a notepad that listens. You open it when a meeting starts, jot down whatever matters to you, and after the call it merges those notes with the transcript into a tidy document. Nothing appears in the participant list, and an optional feature can send participants an automated consent message when a meeting starts.

## What stands out
- The notes read like yours, because your own jottings steer what the AI keeps.
- Chat and Recipes turn a pile of meetings into answers, such as the open action items across a week.
- New companion apps extend it beyond the desktop, including mobile phone calls and an Apple Watch app.

## Limitations
- Granola cannot transcribe pre-recorded files, and it can't isolate audio from a single app.
- Without a bot, speaker names are missing on many platforms.
- Consent is your responsibility: Granola recommends asking permission, and org-wide notification that the app is in use is an Enterprise control.
- Basic hides notes older than 30 days, and monthly billing is the only option below Enterprise.
