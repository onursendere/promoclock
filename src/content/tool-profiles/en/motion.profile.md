## What is Motion?
Motion bills itself as an AI platform for work: projects, tasks, calendar, meetings, docs, notes, dashboards and workflows in one app, built around a scheduler that decides when each task happens. The pitch is that you stop maintaining a plan and let the software rebuild it as things change.

## What stands out
- Scheduling is the core. Tasks carry duration, priority and deadline, so the calendar shows when work will actually happen.
- Projects can be generated from a description, then managed with statuses and progress reports.
- AI features draw on a monthly credit balance per seat, so heavy use is metered rather than unlimited.

## Limitations
- There is no free tier, so the trial is the only way to try Motion at no cost.
- AI credits are finite: 7,500 per seat monthly on Pro AI, and extra credits are billed per 100.
- Billing is per workspace, and each member with full access takes a seat.
- Individual plans cost more per person than team seats, so compare both tabs on the pricing page first.
- Automatic scheduling only helps if you keep durations and deadlines accurate, which takes discipline.
