## What is Recraft?
Recraft is a web-based design studio with iOS and Android apps that share credits and projects with the browser version. It is built around its own models, which output both raster images and editable vectors, and it also offers external image and video models on the same canvas.

## What stands out
- **Real vectors.** The Vector models return editable SVG files, which suits logos, icons and illustrations that must scale cleanly.
- **Design-tool thinking.** Styles, frames, color palettes, mockups and print settings sit right next to generation.
- **Integrations.** Plugins cover Figma, Framer and Google Workspace, and the MCP server lets agents call Recraft directly.

## Limitations
The Free plan is mainly for testing, since its images are public and cannot be used commercially. Monthly subscription credits don't roll over, top-ups are sold only to paid users, and video generation in the mobile app is limited to subscribers. Teams need at least two seats, and each member's monthly credits cannot be shared.
