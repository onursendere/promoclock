## What is Riverside?
Riverside, formerly known as Riverside.fm, is a browser-based studio for recording conversations with remote guests. Instead of capturing a compressed video call, it records each person locally and uploads the files, which keeps audio and video clean even on unstable connections.

## What changed
Riverside has dropped its permanent free plan. New users now start with a 14-day trial of a paid plan, and the company's own guide, updated in September 2026, confirms that no free tier remains.

## Who it's for
Podcasters and YouTube interview shows get the most from Pro. Creators who also stream, send newsletters or run a website fit Grow, and B2B marketing teams hosting webinars need the Webinar plan.

## Limitations
- The trial needs a credit card and converts to a paid plan automatically.
- Separate-track downloads are capped each month below Business.
- Extra editors cost more on Pro, Grow and Webinar, and collaborative editing is Business-only.
- Some AI features consume credits that must be topped up.
