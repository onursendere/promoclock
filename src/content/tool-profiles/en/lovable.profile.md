## What is Lovable?
Lovable is a browser-based builder where you describe an app in plain language and an AI agent writes, tests and publishes it. Projects live in workspaces with unlimited members, and every member draws from the same credit balance.

## Pricing model
Pro and Business are sold in credit tiers from 100 to 10,000 credits a month, from $25 to $2,250 on Pro. Business costs about twice as much per credit and adds SSO, internal publishing and role-based access. Hosting and backend usage also consume credits once the monthly Cloud grant runs out.

## Limitations
- Costs scale with usage, so a busy app or long debugging sessions can outrun a small plan.
- Output is web apps only; there is no React Native or native store build.
- The Free plan's 30 monthly build credits suit experiments, not ongoing projects.
- The pricing page shows local-currency prices in some countries, so the USD figures above may not match what you see.
