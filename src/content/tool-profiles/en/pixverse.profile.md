## What is PixVerse?
PixVerse is a generative video platform built around proprietary models, currently led by PixVerse V6, with C1 for cinematic production and R1 for real-time interactive worlds. The same account also reaches outside video and image models, from Seedance to Nano Banana, billed in one credit currency.

## What stands out
- Credits come from three buckets: daily logins, a 30-day membership allowance and non-expiring bonus packs.
- Higher tiers lower the effective cost per clip through Off-Peak Mode and discounts on partner models.
- Workflow tools go past a prompt box: Agent, Canvas, Marketing Hub and a CLI.

## Limitations
- Free output is limited to 540p with a watermark; 4K output needs Pro or higher.
- Third-party models such as Sora 2 and Kling O3 are for paid plans only.
- Refunds are narrow, and credit packs are non-refundable.
- Membership credits expire every 30 days, so unused allowance is lost.
