## What is Gemini?
Gemini is Google's assistant for chat, research, images and video. You can use it at gemini.google.com, in the Android and iOS apps, and in desktop apps for Mac and Windows. Paid Google AI plans also switch on Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Vids.

## What stands out
- **A plan ladder with storage.** Every tier bundles Google cloud storage, from 15 GB on Free to 5 TB on Pro and at least 20 TB on Ultra.
- **Creative tools in one subscription.** Google Flow credits, Nano Banana image models and Gemini Omni video sit alongside the chat assistant.
- **A cheaper Ultra.** Since May 2026, Ultra starts at $99.99/month for 5x Pro usage instead of only the $199.99 tier.

## Limitations
Limits are compute-based and weekly, so heavy video or Deep Research use can exhaust an allowance quickly. Some features, including Gemini Omni and Gemini in Google apps, need an adult account, and availability varies by country and language. Prices differ by region; the figures above are US prices.
