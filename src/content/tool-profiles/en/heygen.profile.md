## What is HeyGen?
HeyGen makes videos without a camera crew: you type or paste a script, choose a stock or custom avatar, and the platform renders a presenter speaking it. The same account translates existing footage, converts PDFs and slides into video, and exposes these tools through an API. It works in the browser and in iOS and Android apps.

## What stands out
- **Localization at scale.** Voice-cloned translation with lip sync covers 175+ languages and dialects.
- **Long videos on paid tiers.** Creator and Pro allow single videos up to 30 minutes, Business up to 60.
- **Business extras.** SSO, interactive video, SCORM export and LMS integrations arrive on the Business plan.

## Limitations
- Credits, not video counts, limit paid use, and advanced avatar models and lip-synced translation burn them faster.
- 4K export starts on Pro; Creator stops at 1080p.
- The free plan's 1-minute cap and watermark make it a trial rather than a working tier.
- On monthly billing, unused credits expire after one extra billing cycle.
