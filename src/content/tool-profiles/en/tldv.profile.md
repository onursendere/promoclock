## What is tl;dv?
tl;dv is a meeting recorder built around team knowledge. A bot joins Google Meet, Zoom or Teams calls from your calendar, or the desktop app records locally, and each call becomes a video, a translatable transcript and AI notes.

The emphasis is on what happens after the call: notes flow into CRMs and ticketing tools, recurring reports land in your inbox, and Ask AI searches many meetings at once. tl;dv says it partners with Anthropic for its generative AI and never trains AI on customer recordings.

## Who it's for
Sales and customer success teams get the most from Business, which adds playbook monitoring and objection tracking. Smaller teams can stay on Free for recording and move to Pro when the AI caps start to pinch.

## Limitations
- Free keeps data for up to 3 months and caps AI notes at 10 meetings.
- Multi-meeting Ask AI stays at 5 queries on Pro; unlimited use needs Business.
- Web recording is capped weekly, from 40 meetings on Free to 120 on Business.
- Recordings stop at 3 hours on every plan.
- Displayed prices vary by country, so check the currency before you pay.
