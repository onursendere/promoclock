## What is Hailuo AI?
Hailuo AI is the creator-facing app for MiniMax's video models. Since August 2026 its default engine is MiniMax H3, a single model for generating and editing video with sound, while older Hailuo models stay available for other styles and budgets.

## What stands out
- H3 accepts mixed references in one prompt, which helps with brand, product and character consistency.
- Plans are sold as credits, but the pricing page translates them into seconds of video per model and resolution.
- MiniMax released H3 as open weights, so the same model can also be self-hosted.

## Limitations
- There is no recurring free allowance, only one-time trial credits.
- Clip length tops out at 15 seconds, so longer pieces need stitching.
- Only one or two tasks run at a time, depending on plan.
- Displayed discounts change often, so check the checkout price before subscribing.
