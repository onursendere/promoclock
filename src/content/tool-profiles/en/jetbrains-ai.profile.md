## What is JetBrains AI?
JetBrains AI is the umbrella for AI features across JetBrains IDEs, from completion in the editor to agents that change many files. You enable it from the AI Chat window after installing the AI Assistant plugin.

JetBrains now describes a wider ecosystem: Junie, third-party agents over the Agent Client Protocol, the JetBrains Air agentic environment, and JetBrains Central for organization-level governance.

## How the tiers differ
The tiers differ mainly in AI Credits. AI Pro includes credits equal to its price, and AI Ultimate adds a $5 bonus on the individual plan. Organization licenses cost more: $20 for AI Pro and $60 for AI Ultimate.

## Limitations
- 3 monthly credits on AI Free run out after a few agent tasks.
- When the quota is used up, cloud features pause until renewal unless you buy top-ups.
- JetBrains AI is limited to supported territories, and AI Free and AI Ultimate aren't available in Mainland China for now.
- AI Free and bundled AI Pro can't be activated through a License Server.
