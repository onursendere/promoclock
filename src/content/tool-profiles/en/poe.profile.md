## What is Poe?
Poe, made by Quora, is one front end for thousands of AI models and creator-made bots. Instead of paying each AI company separately, you spend points from a single balance on whichever model fits the task.

## What stands out
- **Model variety.** Text models like GPT-5.5 and Claude-Opus-4.7 sit next to image and video bots such as Nano-Banana-Pro and Veo-3.1.
- **Flexible spending.** Points roll over on Plus and above, and add-on points top up a balance without a plan change.
- **Long context.** Paid plans unlock each bot's full context, up to 2M tokens.
- **Everywhere.** Apps cover iOS, Android, Windows and macOS.

## Limitations
Points make costs hard to predict, because premium models and video generation drain a balance much faster than simple chat. Subscriptions aren't sold in every country, and final prices can rise with regional taxes and currency conversion. Free accounts can't call paid models through the API.
