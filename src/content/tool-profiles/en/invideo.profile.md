## What is InVideo AI?
InVideo AI describes itself as the agentic video editor for serious creatives. The product has two halves: an AI agent that plans and generates shots, and a conventional multitrack editor where those shots can be trimmed, graded and mixed. Everything is billed per seat, with credits covering generation.

## What stands out
- **Model choice in one place.** Seedance, Veo, Sora, Kling, Wan, PixVerse and Hailuo sit behind the same credit balance.
- **Hand editing is free.** Only generation consumes credits, so long timeline sessions cost nothing extra.
- **Real collaboration.** Multiplayer projects with live cursors are included from the Starter plan.

## Limitations
Starter is deliberately narrow: Agent Two Lite only, no Seedance 2.5 or 2.0 access, and 400 credits a month. Credits never roll over, and invideo says it may change credit costs at any time without notice, so the amount of video a plan buys can shift. Seats are priced individually, and the free plan is a weekly taster rather than a working tier.
