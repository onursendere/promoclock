## What is Make?
Make builds automations as visual scenarios. Each module in a scenario reads, creates or transforms data in an app, and routers, filters and iterators shape how records flow between them. Scenarios run on a schedule, instantly through webhooks, or as subscenarios called by other scenarios.

## Plans at a glance
Paid tiers are priced by the credits you buy, from 10,000 up to 8 million a month, and every tier includes the same 3,000+ apps.

- **Core** adds unlimited active scenarios, scheduling down to the minute and Make API access.
- **Pro** adds priority execution, custom variables and full-text execution log search.
- **Teams** adds team roles and shared scenario templates; Enterprise adds custom functions and on-prem access.

## Limitations
The Free plan caps scenario runtime at 5 minutes and file size at 5 MB, which rules out heavier jobs, and paid scenarios still stop at 40 minutes. Make AI Agents and AI Web Search were labeled beta on the pricing page in September 2026. A single run can consume anywhere from two credits to thousands, so loops over long lists get expensive fast.
