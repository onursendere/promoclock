## What is OpusClip?
OpusClip is built for one job: repurposing long-form video into short clips. You upload a file or paste a link, and its models pick the moments, trim them, add captions and reframe them vertically in a single pass.

## What stands out
- The credit model is easy to budget because it counts input minutes, not output clips.
- Beyond clipping, OpusClip offers AI Producer for raw footage, a thumbnail generator, an API and an MCP connector for agent workflows.
- Pro teams can schedule a month of posts across several platforms from one dashboard.

## Limitations
- The free plan is really a preview: watermark, no editing and a 3-day export window.
- Starter has no yearly discount, outputs 9:16 only, and its exports expire after 30 days.
- Full API access, CMS integration and priority processing sit on the custom Business plan.
- Clips still need review, since the Virality Score ranks suggestions rather than guaranteeing results.
