## What is MindStudio?
MindStudio sits between simple chatbot builders and developer frameworks. You design an agent's steps visually, choose a model for each step and connect data sources such as websites, SQL databases, vector stores, Google Sheets and CRMs like Salesforce or HubSpot. Agents can generate documents, images, audio and video, and they can ask a human for approval mid-run.

## How pricing works
The subscription unlocks platform features, while AI model tokens are billed separately at the provider's list price. That makes the $20 Individual plan a floor rather than a fixed monthly total. When an account nears its run limit, MindStudio sends a notice, and you can upgrade or buy extra runs.

## Limitations
The Free plan allows a single agent, and building with collaborators requires Business, which is quote-only. Usage charges scale with the models you pick, so agents built on expensive models can cost far more than the subscription. SSO, audit logs, custom domains and self-hosting all need a sales conversation.
