## What is Pixelcut?
Pixelcut started as a set of single-purpose AI tools for background removal, upscaling and retouching. Those tools now sit in one workspace for editing and generating both images and video, available as mobile apps, a browser app and an API.

## What stands out
- **Core edits stay unmetered.** Background removal and upscaling are unlimited on paid plans, so credits are reserved for generative models.
- **A broad model catalog.** The credit table lists dozens of image and video models from Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs, Kling, Runway and others.
- **Agent-ready.** The MCP server and API let coding agents and automated pipelines call exactly the same tools.

## Limitations
Generative Fill, Expand, AI Backgrounds and Pro Eraser are capped at 300 runs a day on Pro. Video models burn credits fast, since a single premium clip can cost hundreds. The free plan only offers limited removal and upscaling, and the commercial license is listed for Pro and Business, not for free accounts.
