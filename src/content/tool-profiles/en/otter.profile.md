## What is Otter.ai?
Otter.ai records, transcribes and summarizes conversations, and since April 2026 has presented itself as a conversational knowledge engine that connects meetings across a team. It works in the browser, on iOS and Android, as a Chrome extension and as a desktop app for Mac and Windows.

## What stands out
- **Coverage beyond scheduled calls.** Desktop and mobile recording capture huddles, lectures and in-person talks, not only calendar meetings.
- **Meeting memory for other AI tools.** The MCP server makes transcripts available to assistants you already use.

## Limitations
The free plan is tight: 30-minute conversations, 3 lifetime imports and a 25-conversation history. Transcription covers only 6 languages. Pro caps usage at 1,200 minutes a month and 90 minutes per meeting, SSO needs a minimum 100-user license, and HIPAA compliance is a paid add-on. Salesforce and HubSpot sync is limited to 1 user on Pro and 5 on Business.
