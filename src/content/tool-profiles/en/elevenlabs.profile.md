## What is ElevenLabs?
ElevenLabs began as a text-to-speech service and now covers most AI audio work in one account. Its products are grouped as ElevenCreative for content tools, ElevenAgents for conversational voice agents and ElevenAPI for developers, all running on one credit balance.

## What stands out
- **Voice range.** The homepage lists 5,000+ voices across 70+ languages, plus cloning of your own voice.
- **One credit pool.** Speech, transcription, music, sound effects and dubbing all draw from the same monthly credits.
- **Output quality tiers.** Pro and above add 192 kbps audio and 44.1 kHz PCM output through the API.

## Limitations
Credits run out quickly on long projects: Creator's 121,000 credits equal roughly 121 minutes of speech, and dubbing burns 2,000 credits per minute. The free plan cannot be used commercially and requires attribution. Rollover only applies while a paid subscription stays active, and team seats start on the $299/month Scale plan.
