## What is Gamma?
Gamma is a browser-based creation tool that treats slides, documents and websites as one flexible format, called a gamma. You describe what you need or paste existing content, choose from more than 100 themes, and the AI produces a first draft you refine card by card.

## What stands out
- **One draft, several outputs.** The same content can be presented, shared as a link, published as a website or exported to PowerPoint.
- **Credits that carry over.** Paid plans refill monthly, and extra credits can be bought.
- **60+ languages.** Prompts and generated content work in more than 60 languages.

## Limitations
Every AI action spends credits, and premium or Ultra image models use the most per image. The free tier is a starter pack, not a monthly allowance, and removing Gamma branding requires Plus. API access starts at Pro, the mobile app is restricted to the US and Canada, and individual workspaces allow AI training on your content unless you opt out.
