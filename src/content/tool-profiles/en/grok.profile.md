## What is Grok?
Grok is SpaceXAI's assistant at grok.com, in the iOS and Android apps and inside X. Beyond chat, one account covers Grok Imagine for images and video, voice conversations, the Grok Build coding agent and, from SuperGrok up, Grok Bot agents. The current plans advertise the Grok 4.6 model.

## How the plans are organized
There are five individual tiers: Free, SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus and SuperGrok Heavy. Paid tiers moved to a single weekly usage pool in June 2026, so a higher tier mainly buys a bigger pool, faster replies and higher video resolution. Grok Bot is also included with paid individual Cursor plans.

## Limitations
- Only SuperGrok and SuperGrok Plus have prices on the public pricing page.
- Every Imagine output carries a watermark, and 720p video falls back to 480p once your tier's 720p cap is reached.
- Companions are available only in the iOS app.
- Extra Usage Credits can be bought only on the web for now, and they expire one year after purchase.
