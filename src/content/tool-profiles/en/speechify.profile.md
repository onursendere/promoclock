## What is Speechify?
Speechify is a listening app: you give it text and it reads it back in a synthetic voice while highlighting the words. It runs in the browser, on iPhone, iPad and Android, on Mac and Windows, and as a Chrome or Edge extension, so a document started on a laptop can continue on a phone.

## What stands out
- **Built for reading, not voiceovers.** Speed control, word highlighting and scanning target people who want to get through text faster.
- **More than a reader.** Premium bundles AI summaries, chat about a document, AI podcasts and voice typing in one subscription.

## Who it's for
Speechify suits students with long reading lists, people who find on-screen reading tiring and professionals who want to review reports hands-free. Creators who need downloadable voiceovers should look at Speechify Studio or a dedicated voice generator instead.

## Limitations
The free plan's robotic voices and 1.5x cap are a steep step down from Premium. Monthly billing at $29 is expensive compared with the yearly price. Speechify does not publish the free trial's length on its pricing page, and Studio and the API cost extra.
