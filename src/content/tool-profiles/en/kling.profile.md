## What is Kling AI?
Kling AI is Kuaishou's generative studio for video, images and sound. It runs on the web at kling.ai, in iOS and Android apps, and in desktop apps for Windows and macOS. Developers can reach the same models through the Kling AI Developer Platform API.

## What stands out
- **Audio in the same pass.** Kling 3.0 generates sound with the picture and can tie a voice to a character's visual identity.
- **Frequent model updates.** The release log shows major launches roughly every one to two months in 2026, including 3.0 Turbo in June.
- **Clear credit value ladder.** The membership page prints the price per 100 credits for every plan.

## Limitations
- Credit costs change with model, resolution, duration and number of outputs, so the same plan buys very different amounts of video.
- Subscription credits expire after 31 days and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash.
- Free Basic output keeps the Kling brand watermark and is limited to one video per generation.
- Monthly plans are advertised with first-month discounts, so check the renewal price before subscribing.
