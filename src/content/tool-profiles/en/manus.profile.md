## What is Manus?
Manus is an autonomous agent: you describe an outcome, and it plans the steps, runs them in a cloud sandbox and returns a report, website, deck or file. It runs on the web, in desktop and mobile apps, and inside your own browser through Browser Operator.

## The Meta deal and its reversal
Meta acquired Manus on December 29, 2025. The takeover was later undone; news outlets including CNBC and Bloomberg reported that Chinese regulators had blocked it. Manus announced its return to independent operations on August 11, 2026 and confirmed it on its blog on September 1.

The separation had real consequences for some users. Data generated after the acquisition date was deleted from affected accounts, and Manus offered backups, a restoration portal, refunds and welcome-back discounts depending on the account type.

## Limitations
- Credit use depends on the tokens, virtual machines and APIs a task needs, so a long job can cost far more than a quick answer.
- The Free plan runs only 1 task at a time.
- Browser Operator is recommended for Chrome and Edge only.
- The 2026 data deletion is worth weighing if you plan to keep long-running projects on the platform.
