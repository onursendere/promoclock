## What is remove.bg?
remove.bg is an automatic background remover that became part of Canva in 2021. It works on the website, in desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux, through an API and via plugins such as Photoshop and Figma.

## The December 2026 shutdown
Canva is folding remove.bg into its own products rather than running two separate tools. From 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET, the website, accounts, self-service API, desktop apps and plugins stop working.

- Single images and bulk batches: Canva's Background Remover, on the web or in the Canva desktop app.
- High-volume automation: Leonardo.ai's API, with a migration guide for self-service users.
- Enterprise API customers: contracts continue unchanged.

## Limitations
- Usage reports and invoices can't be downloaded once accounts close, so export them first.
- Subscription credits never roll over to the next month.
- A new subscription now buys only a few months of service before the closure.
- Canva lists Background Remover as a paid Pro feature, so the replacement isn't a like-for-like credit pack.
