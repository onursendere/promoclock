## What is Grammarly?
Grammarly is a writing assistant that sits on top of the apps you already use and suggests fixes for correctness, clarity and tone. It began as a grammar checker and now includes generative AI, sentence rewrites and a set of task-specific writing agents.

In late 2025, the company renamed itself Superhuman after buying the Superhuman email app, and Coda is part of the same company. The product kept the Grammarly name, but Enterprise customers now buy it through Superhuman Go.

## What stands out
- **Reach.** One account covers browser extensions, Windows and Mac apps, and mobile apps, so the same suggestions follow you everywhere.
- **Published AI limits.** Each plan states its monthly prompt limit instead of hiding usage behind credits.
- **Agents for coursework.** AI Grader estimates a grade against an uploaded rubric.

## Limitations
- Paying month to month costs 2.5 times the annual average of $12.
- AI use stops at 100 prompts a month on Free and 2,000 per member on Pro.
- Plagiarism checks and AI-text detection are Pro features.
- Confidential mode, data loss prevention and BYOK encryption are Enterprise-only, which matters if you work with sensitive text.
