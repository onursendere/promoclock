## What is TurboScribe?
TurboScribe is an upload-and-transcribe service: you drop in an audio or video file, choose the spoken language and mode, and get back a transcript you can edit, translate and export. It accepts common formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 and MOV, and it runs entirely in the browser on desktop and mobile.

## What stands out
- **Flat pricing.** One paid plan covers any amount of transcription for a single user, rather than a per-minute budget.
- **Audio repair.** The optional Restore Audio setting removes background noise from poor recordings, adding 2–3 minutes per hour of audio.

## Limitations
TurboScribe works on uploaded files; the service does not join live meetings or record calls for you. The free plan is capped at 30-minute files, and accuracy depends on the language and audio quality. Unlimited is licensed per person, so account sharing is not allowed, and there are no native desktop or mobile apps.
