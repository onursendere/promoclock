## What is Canva?
Canva is a browser-based design editor with desktop apps for Windows and Mac and mobile apps for iOS and Android. One workspace covers social graphics, presentations, docs, sheets, whiteboards, video, websites and print orders.

At Canva Create 2026 the company introduced Canva AI 2.0, built on its own Canva Design Model, plus Canva Offline for editing without a connection. Canva also owns Affinity, Leonardo.ai and remove.bg, whose background removal is moving into Canva.

## What stands out
- AI tools share one monthly allowance, with Standard, Premium and Ultra tools drawing from it at different rates.
- Business adds 100 Brand Kits, approvals and more AI for small teams, without a minimum seat count for new sign-ups.
- Canva AI works in 17 languages, including English, Spanish, Japanese, Arabic and Hindi.

## Limitations
- AI use counts are estimates. Complex tasks and Ultra tools consume more, so real totals can be lower.
- Prices vary by country: the same Pro plan is shown at £100/year in the UK.
- Monthly billing costs more than the yearly price shown by default.
- Heavy AI users may need AI Pass, which costs far more than the base subscription.
