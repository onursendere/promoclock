## What is Jasper?
Jasper is an AI platform built for marketing work. Instead of a blank chatbot, it surrounds the AI models with brand context such as voices, style guides, audience profiles and product knowledge, and applies it to every draft.

Its homepage now calls it an agent workspace. Canvas handles everyday content, Grid handles scaled production, Studio builds custom apps, and an MCP server connects Jasper's context to other AI tools.

## Who it's for
Jasper suits marketing departments and agencies that need many people to write in one brand voice. Solo marketers can use Pro, but the price assumes professional use.

## Limitations
- No free plan, and Pro is limited to 1 seat and 2 Brand Voices.
- API access, custom agents, Grid, SSO and unlimited brand assets require the custom-priced Business plan.
- Credit-based features make total cost harder to predict when you use GEO, Grid or the API heavily.
- The browser extension supports only Chrome and Edge.
