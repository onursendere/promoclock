## What is Reclaim.ai?
Reclaim.ai is a scheduling layer that sits on top of Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar rather than replacing it. You set priorities, goals and working hours, and its AI agents keep moving flexible events so they fit around fixed meetings.

## What stands out
- **Specialized agents.** Separate Focus Time, Habits, Buffer Time, Smart Meeting and Meeting Quality agents each handle one part of your week.
- **Assistant-friendly Business plan.** A team out-of-office calendar and delegated access for executive assistants come with Business.
- **Fits existing tools.** A free Google Calendar add-on, Slack status sync, Zoom and Raycast integrations connect it to apps you already use.

## Limitations
There are no native iOS or Android apps, only a mobile web app. Lite's 1-week scheduling range and single scheduling link suit light use only, and the Lite team is capped at 1 user. Smart Meetings with 3 or more people can require paid Attendee User packs for participants without a Reclaim seat.
