## What is Luma now?
Luma AI launched Dream Machine as a text- and image-to-video generator, but the brand has since moved on. The current Luma app is an agent-driven creative workspace, and Luma's own guidance says not to use the Dream Machine name for today's product.

## What stands out
- Ray3.2, released June 9, 2026, emphasizes direction over prompting: keyframes, continuity across cuts and HDR output.
- Luma Scenes, added August 11, 2026, lets you fix one shot without re-rendering a whole spot.
- One credit balance spans Luma's models and partner models such as Seedance, Kling, Veo and MiniMax H3.

## Limitations
- There is no free tier, and the entry plan costs $30/month.
- 1080p and HDR renders use credits quickly, so heavy users land on Pro or Ultra.
- The product is aimed at professional workflows, which can feel heavy for quick social clips.
- Android users need to work in the browser.
