## What is Frase?
Frase describes itself as a content operating system for AI search. Its workflow runs as a loop: listen to Search Console data and visitor questions, create content, publish it, monitor Google rankings and AI citations, then fix pages that slip.

## New Frase vs Legacy Frase
Frase rebuilt its product around that loop. The new app lives at next.frase.io, while Legacy Frase stays available at app.frase.io for anyone who has not migrated. Legacy "Rank-Ready Credits" became Article Credits, and credits now reset on your billing date instead of the 1st of the month.

## Limitations
There is no free plan, and Starter is limited to 1 seat, 1 site and 10 articles a month. Reporting history is short on lower tiers: Starter shows current data only and Professional keeps 3 months. Frase also says plainly that no tool will get you ranked or cited inside a 7-day trial, so judge results over weeks, not days.
