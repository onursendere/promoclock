## What is Pika today?
Pika started as a playful text-to-video app known for effects such as Pikaffects and Pikaswaps. By 2026 it has become a broader creative platform with separate apps for video, images and audio, and a model picker that mixes Pika's own models with partner models.

## What stands out
- The pricing page converts credits into output: Creator's 3,150 credits cover roughly 25 Seedance 2.5 clips or 393 Nano Banana 2 images.
- Parallel generation limits scale sharply, from 2 videos on Free and Starter to unlimited on Fancy.
- Developers get a cheaper route to more than 100 models through the API Club membership.

## Limitations
- There is no free allowance, so you pay before generating anything.
- Commercial rights start at $35/month.
- Plan credits expire at the end of each billing period.
- The help center still describes older plan names such as Basic and Standard, so trust the pricing page.
- Pika's iOS app was last updated in July 2025, which makes the web app the main surface.
