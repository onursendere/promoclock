## What is Google Flow?
Google Flow is a browser-based studio where Google's generative models, an AI agent and user-built tools share one project canvas. It targets the whole loop of planning, generating and refining rather than one-off prompts.

## What stands out
- The free tier is unusually usable: 50 credits every day, with access to Veo 3.1, Gemini Omni Flash and Nano Banana Pro.
- Flow credits are bundled into Google AI plans that also include the Gemini app, extra storage and YouTube Premium benefits.
- Tools let you package a workflow, such as shader effects or animated type overlays, then share and remix it.

## Limitations
- You must be 18 or over and in a supported country.
- Video editing and Avatars are blocked in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, and credit purchases are unavailable in Japan.
- Credits never roll over, daily or monthly.
- Prices vary by market, and although the interface covers 38 languages, Google recommends English prompts for best results.
