## What is Freepik (now Magnific)?
Freepik began in 2010 as a search engine for graphic resources and grew into a stock library with a full AI suite. In 2026 the whole platform took the name of its Magnific upscaler, so freepik.com now redirects to magnific.com.

It runs in the browser, in desktop apps for macOS and Windows, and in mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## What stands out
- Stock and AI share one subscription, and credits pay only for AI tools, never for downloads.
- The pricing page lists a credit cost for every model, from Google's Nano Banana models to Kling, Seedance and Veo.
- Annual plans deliver a full year of credits at once, which helps with seasonal peaks.

## Limitations
- Prices are shown in local currency; from the UK the page lists pounds, excluding VAT.
- Magnific reserves the right to change credit costs at any time without notice.
- Monthly credits don't roll over, and extra credit packs are only sold on Premium+ and Pro.
- Unlimited mode covers most image models, not video, and slows down after fair-use limits.
- There's no free trial of paid plans, and the Free plan excludes commercial use.
