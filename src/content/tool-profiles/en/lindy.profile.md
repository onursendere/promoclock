## What is Lindy?
Lindy calls itself an AI employee. Rather than an app you open, it sits in your team's Slack, links to each person's email, calendar and phone number, and returns finished work: answers with sources, drafted replies, meeting notes, scheduled reports and hosted dashboards it builds as artifacts.

## How it is sold
Everyone who uses Lindy takes a seat, including anyone who @mentions it in Slack for the first time. All seats feed one credit pool that the workspace shares, and every plan, trial seats included, can use any of the supported models. Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM, audit logs and HIPAA with a signed BAA.

## Limitations
- There is no free plan, and seats can be created automatically when a colleague mentions Lindy in Slack, so admins need to watch seat counts.
- Plan credits do not roll over; only purchased top-ups carry forward.
- Removing a seat takes effect at the end of the paid cycle, with no mid-cycle proration.
- The shared teammate experience is designed around Slack, with iMessage and the Gmail extension serving the personal assistant instead.
