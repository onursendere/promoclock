## What is Remini?
Remini is a consumer enhancement app operated by AI Creativity S.r.l. in Milan, a Bending Spoons company. It exists as an iOS and Android app and as Remini Web in the browser, and the two products are developed, limited and billed separately.

## What stands out
- **Specialized models.** Different tools handle unblurring, denoising, face detail, backgrounds, color and old prints, rather than one generic filter.
- **Portraits from selfies.** AI Photos turns 8 to 12 uploads into styled shots for profiles, CVs or novelty themes.
- **A commercial route.** Remini Web's Business plan adds commercial use, unlimited enhancements, extra file formats and priority processing.

## Limitations
There is no published price list: offers change between users and over time, and free trials are not always available. Subscribers can still hit daily or per-feature caps, and some outputs keep a watermark by design. The Personal web plan limits enhancements to 20 a week or 80 a month, website subscriptions at yourplan.remini.ai are closed to new users, and the API has been discontinued.
