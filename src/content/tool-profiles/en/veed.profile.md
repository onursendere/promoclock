## What is VEED?
VEED is a browser-based editor that puts a timeline, subtitles, recording and generative AI in one workspace. Projects start from an upload, a screen recording, a template or a text prompt, and every AI generation draws from a shared credit balance.

## What stands out
- Subtitles are the core strength: paid plans include unlimited auto subtitles within fair use, with more caption styles on higher tiers.
- The AI video generator lets you switch between third-party models, so you can trade quality against credit cost per clip.
- Fabric 1.0, VEED's own model, animates any photo, mascot or illustration from a voice track.

## Limitations
- Free exports are watermarked and capped at 720p, and most AI editing tools can only be tried once before upgrading.
- Pricing is per seat and shown in local currency, so team costs grow quickly and figures differ by country.
- Credits reset each billing cycle with no rollover.
- The mobile app is iOS-only for now.
