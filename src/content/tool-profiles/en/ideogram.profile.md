## What is Ideogram?
Ideogram is a generative media platform built around its own image models, with creative apps, an API and MCP access. Its models target design work where words must be legible, such as posters, book covers, merch and ads.

Ideogram 4.0 launched on June 3, 2026 as the company's first open-weight foundation model. It reads prompts as structured JSON, handles 256 to 2048 pixels per side, and runs both as downloadable weights and inside Ideogram.

## What stands out
- Reliable typography, plus Layerize Text for changing copy without re-prompting.
- A model catalog that also includes models from other research labs.
- Enterprise options include legal indemnification, custom SSO and private custom models.

## Limitations
- Older prompts can behave differently with 4.0, which works best with structured, JSON-style prompts.
- Subscription priority credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.
- Custom model training is limited on Pro and Team, with full training reserved for Enterprise.
- The site lists an iPhone app only; no Android app is offered there.
