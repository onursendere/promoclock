## What is Bolt.new?
Bolt.new works entirely in a browser tab, with a live preview of the app next to the chat. You describe what you want, then refine it by prompting, editing code directly or changing text and colors in the preview.

## What stands out
Bolt keeps the whole lifecycle in one tab: build, audit, publish, buy a domain and connect GitHub for version control. Since late August 2026 you can queue up to 20 prompts while it works, and Bolt Slides can build and export presentations.

## Limitations
- Token use grows with project size, and on the Free plan AI building pauses after 300K tokens in a day.
- Forge prompts and code are anonymized and shared with Bolt's partners to train open-source models.
- Projects started for web do not easily convert to mobile, so say "mobile app" in the first prompt.
- Forge is not available on Teams or Enterprise plans.
