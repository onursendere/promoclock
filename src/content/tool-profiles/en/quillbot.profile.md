## What is QuillBot?
QuillBot is a set of writing tools owned by Learneo. Its Paraphraser rewrites sentences in different styles, and one account also gives you grammar checking, summaries, AI detection, humanizing, translation and citations. It works on the web, in browser extensions and in desktop and mobile apps.

## What stands out
- **The Paraphraser.** It is the core tool, with 9 built-in modes, custom modes, adjustable synonyms and paraphrasing in 23 languages on Premium.
- **Low price.** Premium on annual billing costs far less than most AI writing suites.
- **Fewer copy-paste trips.** The Word add-in, extensions and phone keyboards bring the tools into the apps where you write.

## Who it's for
Students, academics and people writing in a second language get the most out of it. Organizations can use the Team Plan, which adds usage metrics and centralized billing.

## Limitations
- The free plan's 125-word limit for paraphrasing and humanizing makes it a sampler, not a daily tool.
- Premium plagiarism checks stop at 25,000 words a month.
- Refunds are limited to what the law requires, so test the free plan first.
