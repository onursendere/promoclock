## What is NotebookLM?
NotebookLM is Google's source-grounded research notebook, now called Gemini Notebook. You create a notebook, add material you trust, and answers point back to quotes from those sources. The old notebooklm.google address now redirects to notebook.google.

## What changed in 2026
- **New name on July 16.** The rename ties the product more closely to the Gemini app and Google Search.
- **Code execution.** Every notebook gained a secure cloud computer for data analysis.
- **Flexible limits on September 2.** Fixed daily caps gave way to a compute budget that refreshes every 5 hours.
- **Study features on September 15.** Real-time voice conversations and a lecture recorder arrived on mobile.

## Limitations
Answers depend on what you upload, so gaps in your sources become gaps in the answers. Heavy users can hit the weekly limit, and the compute budget makes output counts hard to predict. Google AI subscriptions require users aged 18 or older, and prices vary by country.
