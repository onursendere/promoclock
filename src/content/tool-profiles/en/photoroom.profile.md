## What is Photoroom?
Photoroom is an AI editor aimed at product and listing photos rather than general design. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, and work happens inside Spaces, where each Space carries its own subscription and credit pool.

## What stands out
- **Unmetered core edits.** Paid plans include background removal, retouching, AI fill, expanding and enhancing without spending credits, within fair-use limits.
- **Commerce-specific AI.** Ghost mannequin, flat lay, unwrinkling and clothing recolor target fashion and marketplace listings rather than general image generation.
- **Built to scale.** Batch mode, the Shopify integration and the API handle full catalogs instead of single images.

## Limitations
The Free plan is personal use only, so any seller has to pay. Exports pause once the monthly quota runs out, with warnings at 80% and 100%, and the credit allowance can vary by country. A subscription belongs to one Space and does not carry into others, and card payments are the only option on desktop.
