## What is Higgsfield?
Higgsfield is a browser-based creative suite from Higgsfield AI that puts dozens of image and video models behind one credit system. Beyond general generators, it offers studios for specific jobs, including Cinema Studio, Marketing Studio, Lipsync Studio and UGC Factory.

Business plans (Team, Scale and Enterprise) add shared workspaces and pooled credits for teams.

## What stands out
- Camera direction: Cinema Studio lists more than 30 named camera moves that can be stacked within one shot.
- Per-model pricing is public, with credit costs listed per image or per seconds of video.
- Several people can direct one Cinema Studio project and share cast and sets in real time.

## Limitations
- Starter excludes the full Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 models and offers only Seedance 2.0 Fast and Mini.
- Unlimited generations run in the standard queue and can slow down at peak times.
- Fair use rules ban automation, scripting and account sharing on Unlimited.
- There's no free trial period on plans, and prices exclude VAT.
- New models may roll out gradually rather than reaching every user at launch.
