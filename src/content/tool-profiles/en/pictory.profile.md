## What is Pictory?
Pictory is a browser-based video generator aimed at people who have content but not footage. It takes text, a URL, a PowerPoint file, images or an existing recording, writes or follows a script, then assembles scenes from licensed stock media with captions and an AI voiceover. Everything happens in the app; there is nothing to install.

## What stands out
- **Repurposing focus.** Long recordings can be trimmed by text and turned into highlight clips automatically.
- **Licensed media included.** Getty Images, Storyblocks footage and music are covered by the subscription, with no extra royalties.
- **Enterprise hosting.** Pictory Central adds interactive video hosting with chapters, quizzes, CTAs and SCORM export.

## Limitations
Exports, not projects, are the currency: video minutes reset monthly on paid plans and do not roll over, and a single plan covers only one user until the Team tier. Maximum export resolution is 1080p, and the trial caps videos at 5 minutes and 720p. Pictory does not offer refunds on paid subscriptions, and cancelling deletes your projects from the platform for good.
