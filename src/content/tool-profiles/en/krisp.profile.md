## What is Krisp?
Krisp is best known for noise cancellation and has grown into a full meeting assistant. On your computer it acts as the microphone for apps like Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet, cleaning up call audio while it records and transcribes the conversation.

Beyond individual plans, Krisp sells Call Center AI for contact centers and SDKs for developers building voice AI.

## What stands out
- **Audio first.** Noise cancellation and accent conversion work in any calling app, not just the meetings a bot joins.
- **Team features at a low entry price.** Core includes unlimited members, integrations and webhooks.

## Limitations
- Noise cancellation is not available on mobile.
- Accent conversion is time-capped on Core and Advanced.
- Storage is limited to 10 GB on Core and 60 GB on Advanced.
- Salesforce, ConnectWise and manager views require Advanced.
- On-device private transcription, SSO and HIPAA compliance are Enterprise features, and BAA signing is only offered to teams with 100+ seats.
