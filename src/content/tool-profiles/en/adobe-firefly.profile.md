## What is Adobe Firefly?
Adobe Firefly is Adobe's standalone AI studio at firefly.adobe.com and in mobile apps. It brings image, video and audio generation into one place and links to Creative Cloud, so assets can move into Photoshop, Premiere or Adobe Express for finishing.

## What stands out
- **A commercial-use focus.** Adobe trains its own Firefly models on licensed and public-domain content and attaches Content Credentials to outputs.
- **Model choice.** The app advertises more than 30 models, mixing Adobe's image and video models with partner models from Google, OpenAI, Runway and Black Forest Labs.
- **Tight Adobe integration.** Credits and generated assets carry over into the wider Creative Cloud toolset.

## Limitations
Prices differ by country, and credits for video, audio and partner models go quickly: Standard covers up to 20 five-second videos a month. Unused credits do not roll over, commercial-safety claims apply to Adobe's own models rather than partner models, and several newer features launched as public or private betas.
