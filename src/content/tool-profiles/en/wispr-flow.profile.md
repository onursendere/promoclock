## What is Wispr Flow?
Wispr Flow replaces the keyboard with your voice. You speak naturally, and Flow inserts edited text wherever your cursor is, whether that is Slack, Gmail, a code editor or a notes app. It detects 100+ languages automatically.

## What stands out
- **Edits, not just transcripts.** Filler words disappear and punctuation appears, so dictated text rarely needs cleanup.
- **Two tools in one.** Dictation and the Notetaker share one subscription, and notes can feed other AI assistants through MCP.

## Who it's for
Flow suits people who write all day, including founders answering email, support agents and developers prompting AI coding tools. Teams can share a dictionary and snippets on Pro, while Growth adds SSO and HIPAA enforcement with a signed BAA.

## Limitations
Free users hit the weekly word limit quickly with daily use. Everything is processed in the cloud, so Flow needs a connection and may not suit strict offline policies. The Notetaker is Mac and Windows only for now.
