## What is Suno?
Suno turns a written idea into a finished song with singing, lyrics and backing music. It runs in the browser and in mobile apps for iOS and Android, where you can also record your own voice or a melody as a starting point.

## What changed in 2026
Two changes reshaped Suno in September 2026. From September 3, downloads became capped per plan, and free accounts lost regular monthly downloads. On September 9, Suno released v6, its first model family built with licensed music from industry partners, and retired every earlier model.

## Who it's for
Casual users can experiment every day on the free credits. Creators who publish music on YouTube, social media or in podcasts need Pro or Premier for commercial rights, and producers who want MIDI and effects work belong on Premier for Suno Studio.

## Limitations
- The best models, stems and commercial use all require a paid plan.
- Download caps now apply to every song in your library, including ones made before September 3, 2026.
- Credits reset each day or month, and extra downloads cost more.
- Commercial use remains subject to limits in Suno's terms of service.
