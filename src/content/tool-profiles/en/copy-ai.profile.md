## What is Copy.ai?
Copy.ai is an AI platform for go-to-market teams. It pairs a chat that uses several AI models with workflows that combine research, writing and data steps into processes the whole team can reuse.

Fullcast has owned Copy.ai since October 2025. Help articles now live on Fullcast's support site, and Fullcast sells the product as part of its revenue operations suite.

## Who it's for
Small teams that mostly want shared AI chat can start on the Chat plan, which includes 5 seats. The credit-based tiers are for larger revenue organizations that want to automate prospecting, lead handling and content production.

## Limitations
- There is no free plan or published free trial on the current pricing page.
- The next plan after Chat at $29 is Growth at $1,000 per month, so there is no middle tier.
- Credit use depends on how complex a workflow is, which makes costs hard to predict.
- API access is listed only on Enterprise.
