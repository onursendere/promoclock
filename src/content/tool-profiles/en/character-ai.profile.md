## What is Character.AI?
Character.AI is built around user-made Characters rather than a single general assistant. You pick or write a Character, choose a chat style, and memory pins and Lorebook entries keep the story on track.

In 2026 it moved further into entertainment with c.ai Series episodes, a Feed and Comics.

## Who it's for
Adults who enjoy role-play, collaborative fiction and fandom get the most from it. For research, coding or office work, a general-purpose assistant is a better fit.

## Limitations
- Open-ended chat is limited to users 18 and over; under-18 accounts get creative formats instead, and parents can connect a Parental Insights tool for weekly email updates.
- The Free plan shows ads and uses slow mode, and the best memory tier needs c.ai+.
- Comics run on Charms, so spending can go beyond the subscription.
- Characters produce fiction and can state things that aren't true.
