## What is Sudowrite?
Sudowrite is an editor built for long-form fiction rather than emails or marketing copy. Each project holds your manuscript, a Story Bible and a history of generated cards, so you can compare AI suggestions side by side before inserting any of them.

## Plans and credits
All three plans include the same toolkit, Muse included, and differ mainly in monthly credits:
- **Hobby & Student**: 225,000 credits, pitched at people writing for fun or for school.
- **Professional**: the most popular tier, aimed at novels and screenplays.
- **Max**: 2,000,000 credits that roll over for 12 months.

## Limitations
Credit use is hard to predict because each model burns credits at a different rate; in August 2026 Sudowrite cut GPT-5.6 Luna's credit use by 70%, for example. Features other than Write may answer in English when you write in another language. Muse is marketed as having no content filters, which suits dark themes but not every setting. The app is built for storytelling, so business and academic writers are better served elsewhere.
