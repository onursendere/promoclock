## What is Midjourney?
Midjourney is a subscription-only generator for images and short videos. You write prompts on midjourney.com or send them to the Midjourney Bot in Discord, and control results with parameters such as --v for version or --hd for 2K output.

The V8 series arrived in 2026: V8.0 as an alpha on March 17, V8.1 on April 14 and V8.2 as the default on July 24. V8.1 renders standard jobs about 4–5 times faster than earlier versions.

## What stands out
- Image quality and style: results tend to look finished with short prompts, and personalization sharpens that further.
- Niji 7, released on January 9, 2026, covers anime and illustrative styles with cleaner line work.
- Plans are simple: every tier is the same product, scaled by GPU time and privacy.

## Limitations
- There's no free plan and no trial on the main website.
- Basic includes just 200 minutes of Fast GPU time a month and no Relax mode.
- Private work needs Pro or Mega, which starts at $60/month.
- Pan, Zoom Out and region edits on HD images return SD results until you upscale them again.
