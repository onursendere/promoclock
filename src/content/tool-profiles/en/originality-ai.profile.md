## What is Originality.ai?
Originality.ai is a web app for checking content before it is published or graded. You paste text, upload Word or PDF files, or enter a URL, and it returns AI and plagiarism results that you can download or share as read-only reports.

## How Pro and Enterprise differ
Both paid plans share the same core tools; the differences are volume, history and support.
- **Pro**: 2,000 credits a month, 30 days of scan history, team management, extra seats at $9.95/month.
- **Enterprise**: 15,000 credits, 365 days of history, API access, extra seats at $24.95/month.

## Limitations
AI detection is probabilistic. Originality.ai itself notes that AI editing tools, including some Grammarly features, can cause false positives and that short texts reduce accuracy. Monthly subscription credits do not roll over, plagiarism checks require a paid plan, and paste-ins to the free detector may feed model training unless you have an account and opt out.
