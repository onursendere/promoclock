## What is Surfer?
Surfer, found at surferseo.com, is a web app for planning, writing and optimizing content for Google and AI answer engines. Its Content Editor compares your draft with pages that already rank and suggests topics to cover, a structure and a target length.

Positive acquired Surfer in October 2025. Surfer says its founding team and day-to-day operations are unchanged, and its web pages now use the Positive Surfer name.

## What stands out
- **One score to improve.** The Content Score gives writers a single number to raise while they draft.
- **AI visibility and SEO together.** AI Tracker adds brand tracking in ChatGPT and other AI models next to the classic ranking tools.
- **Work where writers are.** The Google Docs extension and WordPress plugin mean nobody has to switch into a new editor.

## Limitations
- There is no free plan, and monthly prices are well above the yearly rates.
- On monthly billing, Discovery allows only 10 documents a month and no AI prompt tracking.
- Standard's AI tracking covers only ChatGPT and refreshes weekly.
- API access requires Peace of Mind, which costs $359 a month or $299 a month billed yearly.
