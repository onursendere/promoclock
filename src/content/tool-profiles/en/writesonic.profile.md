## What is Writesonic?
Writesonic is a web platform for generative engine optimization (GEO) and SEO. It checks real AI answers for the prompts you care about and shows where competitors are recommended instead of you. It then gives you content, audit and agent tools to fix those gaps.

## What stands out
- **Tracking and fixes in one place.** You don't need a separate monitoring dashboard and a separate AI writer.
- **Clear limits.** The pricing page lists tracked prompts, answers per day, articles, audits and pages for every tier.
- **Reporting links.** Google Search Console, GA4 and WordPress integrations tie AI visibility to the SEO data you already track.

## Limitations
- Plans start at $99 a month, which is a lot for a solo blogger who only needs an AI writer.
- Self-serve plans track only 3 AI surfaces. Perplexity, Claude and Copilot require Enterprise.
- Full Action Center access is Enterprise-only, and lower plans get only trial runs of the agents.
- A fair usage policy applies to all plans.
