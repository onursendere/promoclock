## What is Fireflies.ai?
Fireflies.ai turns meetings into searchable transcripts, summaries and task lists. You connect a calendar and the notetaker joins scheduled calls, or you capture audio with the Chrome extension, the iOS and Android apps, the desktop app or a file upload.

The product now reaches past note-taking. The Fireflies site lists Live Assist for real-time suggestions during calls, an Email Assistant, Voice Agents and an MCP server that brings meeting data into assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT.

## Who it's for
Fireflies suits teams that hold many external calls and want the output inside other software, with 50+ integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack and Asana. Business adds team analytics and conversation intelligence for managers who review calls in bulk.

## Limitations
- Free storage stops at 400 minutes, and AI summaries on Free are limited.
- Pro storage is capped at 8,000 minutes per seat; unlimited storage starts on Business.
- Uploads, API transfers and dialer calls share a monthly cap of 3,000 minutes per source for individuals, with overage billed at $0.01 per minute.
- Every workspace member counts as a paid seat on paid plans.
- Some guests dislike a bot in the call; the desktop app avoids that but keeps no recording file.
