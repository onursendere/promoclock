## What is Descript?
Descript turns a recording into a transcript you can edit like a Word document. Deleting a sentence removes it from the video and audio, and moving a paragraph reorders the footage. On top of that sits Underlord, an AI co-editor that can make a first cut, pull clips, write captions and apply effects from a prompt.

## What stands out
- **One tool for the whole show.** Recording, transcription, multitrack editing, effects and publishing live in the same project.
- **Predictable AI costs on the basics.** Studio Sound and filler-word removal are capped at 30 credits per use.
- **Team-ready.** Creator scales to 3 people and Business to 5, with Brand Studio and priority support at the top.

## Limitations
Usage is metered twice: media minutes for what you import or record, and AI credits for anything generative. Neither rolls over, and top-ups are only available from Creator upwards. Free exports are 720p with a watermark, and the free AI credit allowance is a single 100-credit grant that never refills. There is no native mobile app, so editing happens on a desktop or in the browser.
