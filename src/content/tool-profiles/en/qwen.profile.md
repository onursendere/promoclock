## What is Qwen Studio?
Qwen Studio is the new name for Qwen Chat, Alibaba's consumer assistant for its Qwen model family. It lives at chat.qwen.ai and in apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The app doubles as a showcase for models that developers reach through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio. Alibaba also publishes some Qwen models with open weights, such as Qwen3.8-27B, for local use.

## What stands out
- **Price.** Chat, image generation and document analysis cost nothing, with no upsell tier listed.
- **Multimodal input.** Text, images, audio and video go into the same conversation.
- **Long context.** Qwen3.7-Plus accepts up to 1 million tokens through the API, which helps with large files.

## Limitations
Qwen Studio doesn't publish usage limits for free accounts. The iOS app wasn't available in the US or German App Store when checked in September 2026. Alibaba doesn't name the default chat model on an official page, so check the model picker. Check your company's data rules before uploading confidential files to a free cloud chatbot.
